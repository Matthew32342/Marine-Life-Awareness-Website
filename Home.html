Protecting Marine Life

At Marine Life Awareness we strive to spread awareness about the threats marine life face and encourage people to take action to protect our oceans. This informative website explores issues such as plastic pollution, overfishing, and habitat destruction, while highlighting endangered species including sea turtles, Great Hammerhead Sharks, and North Atlantic Right Whales.
